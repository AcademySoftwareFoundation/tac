    - Both 2D and 3D content, represents ASWF projects: OpenEXR, OpenUSD, OpenVDB, ACES...
    - Can be useful for other projects like ORI, DNA...
  - License
    - ASWF Digital Assets License v1.1
    - All assets under the same license
    - In consultation with legal representatives of member companies
    - solely for education, training, research, software and hardware development, performance benchmarking ... software and hardware demonstrations
  - ALab - Gaussian Splats
    - Gaussian Splat variant of ALAb asset
    - Developed by Netflix Animation Studios
    - Contains original PLY and derived USD
    - Coordinated with development of new UsdVol ParticleField Schema domain and renderer from AOUSD and Apple
    - Also provides source SDR/HDR renders and COLMAP data
  - ASC StEM v3
    - Reference material for Virtual Production / ICVFX
    - From ASCI MITC Join Committee on Virtual Production
    - Mix of 2D & 3D assets and short film
    - Contributions from numerous studios (11 sub assets)
    - Shot at Amazon Studios in Los Angeles
  - MaterialX Teapot And Lion

    - Demonstrates modular multi-layer networks with blended inter-layer effects using standalone BSDFs
    - Originally authored to showcase neural materials
